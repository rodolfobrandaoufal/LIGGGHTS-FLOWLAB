:line

gran cohesion dmt model :h3

[Syntax:]

cohesion dmt :pre

zero or more keyword/value pairs may be appended to the end (after all models are specified) :l
  {tangential_reduce} values = 'on' or 'off'
    on = the Coulomb limit of the tangential (and rolling) model sees F_Hertz - F_po + 2 F_po
    off = the tangential model sees the Hertz normal force only :pre

[Examples:]

fix m5 all property/global workOfAdhesion peratomtypepair 1 0.05
pair_style gran model hertz tangential history cohesion dmt :pre

[Description:]

Derjaguin-Muller-Toporov (DMT, 1975) adhesion for stiff, weakly adhesive
particles, for use with the "hertz"_gran_model_hertz.html normal model in
"pair gran"_pair_gran.html and "fix wall/gran"_fix_wall_gran.html. While the
surfaces overlap (delta >= 0) a constant attractive force

F_adh = -2*pi*w*R* :pre

is added to the Hertz force, where w is the work of adhesion and
R* = Ri*Rj/(Ri+Rj) (R* = Ri for a wall). The pull-off force is 2*pi*w*R*.
The force acts in contact only: there is no attraction outside contact and
no hysteresis. w is read exactly as for "cohesion jkr"_gran_cohesion_jkr.html
({workOfAdhesion} peratomtypepair / peratomtype with w_ij = sqrt(w_i*w_j) /
scalar, or {surfaceEnergy} peratomtypepair).

[Verification:]

tests/adhesion/run_all.sh checks the force-overlap curve (relative error
< 1e-6) and the pull-off force 2*pi*w*R* (< 2 %) for pair and wall contacts.

[Restrictions:]

Normal model {hertz} only; spherical particles only; no coarse-graining.

[Related commands:]

"gran cohesion jkr"_gran_cohesion_jkr.html, "gran model hertz"_gran_model_hertz.html

[Default:]

{tangential_reduce} = 'off'

:line

[(Derjaguin 1975)] B.V. Derjaguin, V.M. Muller, Yu.P. Toporov, J. Colloid Interface Sci. 53 (1975) 314-326.
