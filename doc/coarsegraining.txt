:line

coarsegraining command :h3

[Syntax:]

coarsegraining factor keyword value :pre

factor = coarse-graining factor alpha >= 1 :ulb,l
zero or one keyword/value pair may be appended :l
keyword = {model_check} :l
  {model_check} value = {error} or {warn}
    error = stop if a model is used that is not consistent with coarse-graining (default)
    warn = print a warning instead :pre
:ule

[Examples:]

coarsegraining 2.0
coarsegraining 3.0 model_check warn :pre

[Description:]

Coarse-graining represents alpha^3 original particles of radius r by one
particle of radius alpha*r with the same material properties, so that a
system of N particles is simulated with about N/alpha^3 particles (the
time step can grow with alpha as well, since the Rayleigh time scales
with the radius). This command sets alpha:

the radius given in "fix particletemplate/sphere"_fix_particletemplate_sphere.html
({radius constant}) and in "set"_set.html {diameter} is multiplied by alpha, :ulb,l
where the per-type-pair properties {kn}, {gamman} and {gamman_abs} are
read, they are multiplied by alpha, 1/alpha and alpha^2 (the models that
read them are nevertheless flagged by the model check below), :l
models and commands whose results change with alpha stop with an
error, or warn with {model_check warn}: the hooke, hooke/hysteresis,
hooke/stiffness, hertz/stiffness and luding normal models, all cohesion
models, the rolling friction models, "create_atoms"_create_atoms.html
(it places particles without scaling), and the fixes addforce, buoyancy,
drag, efield, spring, spring/rg, spring/self and viscous. :l,ule

The hertz normal model with tangential history needs no scaling: with
the same Young's modulus, Poisson ratio, restitution and friction the
contact stresses, and hence the bulk stresses of a dense packing, do not
depend on the particle size.

Test (tests/cg): a bed made by inserting a solid volume fraction of 0.25
of particles with radius 1 mm and letting it settle under gravity
(hertz, tangential history, mu = 0.5, e = 0.3) was repeated with
"coarsegraining 2" (radius 2 mm). The mass was equal to 1e-4 and the
height of the centre of mass agreed to 1.2 % (10313 against 1289
particles).

The command must be given before the simulation box is defined (before
"create_box"_create_box.html, "read_data"_read_data.html or
"read_restart"_read_restart.html); the factor is not stored in restart
files, so repeat the command before read_restart.

[Restrictions:]

One factor for all atom types. Coarse-graining is an approximation: flows
in which the number of particles across a gap, the particle size
relative to walls, or individual collisions matter are not reproduced.

[Related commands:]

"fix particletemplate/sphere"_fix_particletemplate_sphere.html, "set"_set.html

[Default:]

No coarse-graining (alpha = 1); model_check error.
