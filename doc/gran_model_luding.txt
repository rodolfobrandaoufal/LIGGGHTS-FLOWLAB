:line

gran model luding model :h3

[Syntax:]

model luding \[other model_type/model_name pairs as described "here"_pair_gran.html \] keyword values :pre
zero or more keyword/value pairs may be appended to the end (after all models are specified) :l
  {limitForce} values = 'on' or 'off'
    on = the normal force is never attractive when coefficientAdhesionStiffness and pullOffForce are zero (default)
    off = the dashpot may produce an attractive force at the end of a contact
  {tangential_damping} values = 'on' or 'off'
    on = activates tangential damping (default)
    off = no tangential damping
  {correctRestitution} values = 'on' or 'off'
    on = choose the viscous damping per contact so that hysteresis plus dashpot give the input coefficient of restitution (see below)
    off = the dashpot is computed from coefficientRestitution alone and acts on top of the hysteresis (default) :pre

[Examples:]

pair_style gran model luding tangential no_history
pair_style gran model luding tangential no_history correctRestitution on :pre

[Description:]

Hysteretic linear elastic-plastic-adhesive normal contact of
"(Luding)"_#Luding2008: loading with stiffness k1 (LoadingStiffness), un- and
reloading with k2 that grows from k1 to k2max = kn2k1*k1 (UnloadingStiffness)
as the maximum overlap approaches the plasticity limit
deltaMaxLim = k2max/(k2max-k1) * phiF * 2 R* (coefficientPlasticityDepth phiF),
adhesive branch -kc*delta (kc = kn2kc*k1, coefficientAdhesionStiffness) and a
constant pull-off force (pullOffForce). A linear dashpot with damping
gamma_n = 2 zeta sqrt(m* k1) is added.

[Coefficient of restitution:]

The hysteresis alone already dissipates energy: without the dashpot a head-on
collision below the plasticity limit gives e = sqrt(k1/k2)
"(Luding)"_#Luding2008, which depends on the impact velocity through k2(deltaMax).
With {correctRestitution} = 'off' (default) the dashpot is computed from
coefficientRestitution e as for a linear spring k1 (zeta = -ln e/sqrt(ln^2 e + pi^2)),
so both dissipation mechanisms stack and, with limitForce on, the realised
restitution differs from the input. Example (e = 0.5, deltaMaxLim = 1.05 times the
elastic maximum overlap): realised e = 0.550 (kn2k1 = 1), 0.449 (kn2k1 = 2),
0.352 (kn2k1 = 4).

With {correctRestitution} = 'on' the damping ratio of each contact is chosen at
first touch from the normal approach speed v0 such that the collision gives the
input e. In units k1 = m* = deltaMaxLim = 1 the collision depends only on
kn2k1, zeta and s = v0 / (sqrt(k1/m*) deltaMaxLim); zeta(s) is tabulated once per
type pair (64 points per decade, s = 1e-4 ... 1e4, RK4 integration of the clipped
law) and the value for the contact is stored in the contact history until the
contact opens. If the hysteresis alone already dissipates more than requested
(sqrt(k1/k2) < e), zeta = 0 and the realised restitution is the hysteretic one.
For kn2k1 = 1 the law is the linear spring-dashpot and the closed form of
"model hooke"_gran_model_hooke.html is used. Measured head-on collisions
(sphere-sphere and sphere-wall, kn2k1 = 1, 2, 4, s = 0.3, 0.95, 3,
e = 0.3, 0.5, 0.8): |e_out - e_target| <= 5.9e-3 at dt = t_c/50 and <= 2.8e-3 at
dt = t_c/100 (t_c = pi sqrt(m*/k2max)); the error is the first-order timestep
error of the dashpot (see "model hooke"_gran_model_hooke.html).

In dense, long-lasting contacts the "impact velocity" is the approach speed at
the first step of overlap; the damping of that contact stays fixed until it
opens.

[Restrictions:]

{correctRestitution} = 'on' requires {limitForce} = 'on' and kn2k1 >= 1. For
type pairs with coefficientAdhesionStiffness or pullOffForce different from zero
(the force is then not clipped) it is ignored with a warning.
{correctRestitution} adds one contact-history value: restart files written with
it must be read with the same setting.
Tangential damping uses the uncorrected coefficient.

[Default:]

{limitForce} = 'on'
{tangential_damping} = 'on'
{correctRestitution} = 'off'
